Hi there

I've found some great resources for data structures and algorithms, and I think I should share them with you.

First up is [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/), which I think is a great resource if you already have a good grasp of the topics. If you'd rather learn each topic step by step and solve a variety of questions, check out [Striver's SDE Sheet](https://takeuforward.org/prep-hub/strivers-180-master-dsa-patterns).

If this is your first time working through Striver's SDE Sheet and you prefer reading over watching videos, I'll be starting a blog series that shares solution approaches for each question in the sheet, from brute force to optimal (inspired by Striver's videos).

For dynamic programming, I recommend [Aditya Verma's playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL_z_8CaSLPWekqhdCPmFohncHwz8TY2Go).

If you're looking for notes on all the topics in one place, [here's a Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zRsMuBtZHsTNxFBS4Y05lqA79gl4g1BO?fbclid=PAcGRvZgJleHRuA2FlbQMxMDAAc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQPOTM2NjE5NzQzMzkyNDU5AAGnObG7zwLl1VfoeoB2P8P7zhISYLOEzeh3FCgrmNssFYbh5ghn50Y8ZkMhh6s_aem_4HfcLQ4pC_5sW4va__CK0A) with everything together.

And here's one fun thing I found: pick a random flight on [Flightradar24](https://www.flightradar24.com/), book a seat online with random people on [Focus Town](https://www.focustown.app/), and study until the flight lands.

Thanks for reading :)
